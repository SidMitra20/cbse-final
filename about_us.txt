========================================
             ABOUT AIRBNB               
========================================

Welcome to the Airbnb Vacation Rental App!

Our platform connects travelers with unique homes, 
cozy apartments, oceanfront villas, and mountain chalets 
all around the globe.

Key Features of Our System:
- Explore top destinations (City, Mountains, Beaches, Island)
- View stay details and real-time availability
- Book stays with date validation & database persistence
- Built with Python Tkinter, MySQL, and File Handling

Developed for CBSE Class 12 Computer Science Project.
